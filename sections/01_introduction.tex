\section{Introduction} \label{sec:intro}

This section describes the structure of \textbf{Aethelgard Studios} and its business model.

\subsection{Company Overview}
Aethelgard Studios is an independent start-up game development and publishing company established to build and operate high-scale,
online multiplayer experiences.
Operating as a digital studio, the company integrates internal game development capabilities with online operational management,
dynamic system controls, and a community-driven creator marketplace ecosystem.

\subsection{Main Product Offering}
The studio's flagship offering is \textit{Aethelgard: The Shifting Realms}, a freemium\footnote{A freemium model provides basic
features or initial access free of charge, while requiring payment for advanced content, progression tiers, or premium digital
goods.} Massively Multiplayer Online\footnote{MMO is a type of online video game that allows a large number of players to
simultaneously interact in the same game server.} Action Role-Playing Game\footnote{RPG comprise a broad video game genre generally
defined by a detailed story and character advancement (often through increasing characters' levels or other skills). Role-playing
games almost always feature combat as a defining feature and traditionally used turn-based combat; however, modern role-playing
games commonly feature real-time action combat or even non-violent forms of conflict resolution} (MMO-ARPG).

The game combines action combat with a deeply interconnected, player-driven economy.
Players form guilds, navigate procedurally evolving wilderness zones, challenge complex boss encounters, and trade player-crafted
items across regional market hubs.

\subsubsection{Unique Selling Proposition}
What distinguishes Aethelgard from traditional MMORPGs is its Player-Driven Ecosystem and Dynamic World-Building.
Rather than existing in a static virtual world, every economic transaction, territorial claim, and guild milestone directly
influences the evolving game environment.
Putting permanent, meaningful influence into the hands of player guilds and traders creates a living, community-shaped world where
strategic decisions carry genuine long term consequences.

\subsection{Market and Customers}
Aethelgard Studios targets a core demographic of gamers aged 20--40 located primarily in North America, Western Europe and Central
Asia.

The total addressable audience is divided into three primary player segments that sustain the social structure and economy of the
game:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{General Players (Core Audience):} The foundational player base driven by action-RPG combat, character progression,
  dungeon crawling, and dynamic world exploration. These players drive day-to-day engagement and form the primary market for
  cosmetic assets and expansion events.
  \item \textbf{Organizers (Community Leaders):} High-engagement players who manage guilds, coordinate territorial strategies,
  and oversee regional market hubs. Active and subscribed Organizers who achieve specific mastery thresholds earn Aethelgard-funded
  stipends for sustaining community infrastructure.
  \item \textbf{Creators (Developers):} Content creators who build and publish custom cosmetic assets and guildhall cosmetic
  templates to the integrated Creator Marketplace. Creators earn real-world revenue by monetizing their designs to Organizers and
  General Players, continually expanding the game's visual variety.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Business Strategy}
Aethelgard Studios employs a Freemium Business Model with an Integrated Creator Marketplace and ISP distribution partnerships.

\subsubsection{Free-to-Play Core}

Access to the base game, map, and core character power progression is completely free, removing initial barriers to entry.
Player housing and guild halls are available to every player at no cost; only their customization is sold.
Every purchase is account-wide, so a cosmetic, a housing item or a Patron perk can be used by all characters on the player's
account, which encourages players to create and keep several characters.

\paragraph{Fairness Guardrails}
The only link between real money and gold is the Patron Token. Five safeguards control it:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Status only:} A Patron Token unlocks Patron status and nothing else.
  \item \textbf{Weekly limit:} Each account may buy up to 5 and sell up to 5 tokens per week. The Studio reviews this limit every
  quarter and adjusts it based on trading data.
  \item \textbf{Unbuyable top gear:} The strongest crafted gear requires materials that drop from boss encounters and territory
  control and cannot be bought, so gold alone does not buy top power.
  \item \textbf{AI fraud detection:} The AI system (\autoref{sec:intro}) monitors token trading for gold farming and account
  abuse.
  \item \textbf{Transparency and correction:} The Studio publishes quarterly monetisation transparency reports. If accounts that
  acquire gold through tokens hold more than twice their share of the active player base in top-tier gear, the Studio lowers the
  weekly token limit.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Distribution Strategy}
User acquisition is driven primarily through proprietary launcher channels (maximizing direct revenue without platform fees)
alongside third-party storefronts such as Steam and the Epic Games Store to maximize reach.

\subsubsection{ISP Partnerships}
To streamline player conversions and reduce payment friction, Aethelgard Studios integrates its payment architecture directly with
major Internet Service Providers (ISPs).
Players can settle recurring subscriptions, level expansion purchases, and live event entry fees directly through their monthly
ISP utility bill.
Additionally, strategic ISP bundling allows providers to offer exclusive game access passes and cosmetic bundles to their
high-bandwidth subscribers.

\subsection{AI Integration}
Artificial intelligence is integrated strictly as an operational support infrastructure rather than a core gameplay component,
adhering to high standards of operational integrity and player safety.

Its scope encompasses three primary areas:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Providing automated, 24/7 multi-tiered customer service resolution to handle player inquiries efficiently.
  \item Powering real-time anti-cheat detection systems to identify dynamic exploits, memory tampering, and
  unauthorized third-party software.
  \item Conducting automated analysis of in-game text and voice player interactions to detect toxicity, harassment, and fraud
  patterns for proactive moderation.
\end{itemize}
